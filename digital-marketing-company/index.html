How we run your ads

  1. 01

    Key words

    Our approach to digital marketing is transparent. We find the most relevant keywords for your business and create a list of hundreds and/or thousands of keywords, all relevant to what customers would search to find and locate your business. We add negative or positive keywords to help ensure our clients only receive top-notch traffic from our Google Ads campaigns and search partners.

  2. 02

    Multiple approaches

    We don't like to just create one campaign with one approach. We generate a plethora of campaigns, all of which use different ads and parameters, to make sure we're getting as much valuable data as possible in our evaluations.

  3. 03

    Daily evaluation

    Every day a team member evaluates your marketing campaigns and determines which keywords benefit the business best in conversions, while also being cost-effective. We then add or remove keywords accordingly so our customers get the best results daily. We inspect things such as click-through rate, cost, conversions and impressions.

  4. 04

    Consulting

    After gathering information and data, we share our findings and results with you in a way that is clearly understandable.

Remote computer support

Reliable help from our technicians, wherever you are in the United States. Follow the steps below to get connected.

Please note

Remote computer support sessions are non-refundable. Please check with a team member before proceeding. Thank you.

  1. Step 1

    Talk to a team member

    Call or message us first so we can confirm what you need and answer any questions before your session.

    Call (561) 866-3035 Send a message

  2. Step 2

    Download Quick Assist

    Quick Assist is Microsoft's remote help tool for Windows. It lets a technician see your screen and help fix the problem while you stay in control.

    Quick Assist download

  3. Step 3

    Check out

    Pay for your remote support session through our online checkout.

    Remote support checkout

  4. Step 4

    We connect and get to work

    Your technician will walk you through getting connected, then work on your computer while you watch. You can end the session at any time.

Quick Assist works on Windows 10 and 11. Using a Mac or another device? Call us and we'll tell you the best way to connect.
